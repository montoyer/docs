**1. (Q91)** Before deploying a new data analytics tool for audit use, the first matters to settle are:

* **A.** The licensing cost and the number of user seats.
* **B.** The visual presentation of the outputs in reports.
* **C.** The training schedule for the whole audit function.
* **D.** Reliable access to the source data, the quality of that data, and validation that the tool's logic produces correct and reproducible results.

<details>

<summary> <strong>D</strong> — Reliable data access, data quality, and validation that the tool's logic gives correct and reproducible results all precede rollout, training and presentation.</summary>
